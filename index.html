FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know before visiting us.

Do you offer takeaway coffee?

Yes, all our beverages and food items are available in eco-friendly takeaway packaging.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes, our menu features a wide selection of vegetarian sandwiches, pastas, bowls, and bakery items.

Is Wi-Fi available?

Complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi is available for all dine-in guests looking to work or study.

Can I work from the café?

Absolutely! Many of our guests enjoy working from Aurelia during daytime hours. We provide ample seating and power outlets.

Do you accept reservations?

We operate primarily on a walk-in basis to maintain a relaxed, open atmosphere for everyone.

What are your opening hours?

We are open Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM, and Saturday to Sunday from 8:00 AM to 11:00 PM.

Do you offer dairy-free milk options?

Yes, we offer oat milk and almond milk alternatives for all coffee and tea beverages.
